## Content rules

- Keep reference prose free of personal names, account details, source-site branding, testimonials, user comments, and scraped navigation.
- Use anonymous actors such as buyer A, buyer B, a worker, or a candidate. Retain necessary technology and established algorithm names.
- Do not infer completed lessons or CURRENT depth from pasted reading material, account percentages, or a practice title.
- Distinguish supplied concepts, independently checked mechanisms, illustrative assumptions, and missing material. A menu item is not the contents of the linked lesson.
- Preserve existing domain scope. Do not invent detailed material for a technology just because its name appeared in navigation.
- Correct demonstrable mistakes before teaching. Treat benchmarks, hard limits, prices, and provider-specific feature claims as version-dependent evidence.
- Prefer primary technical documentation for validation. Use impersonal link labels; do not import source authors or identities into examples.
