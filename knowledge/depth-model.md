# Depth Model

D0 Recognition — heard of it.
D1 Definition — explains what and why.
D2 Mechanism — explains how and can implement/use it.
D3 Production Usage — applies it in realistic systems.
D4 Failure Reasoning — handles edge cases, outages, races, overload, recovery.
D5 Scale & Trade-offs — quantitative and architectural reasoning at scale.
D6 Architecture & Evolution — multi-region, migrations, governance, long-term evolution, second-order effects.

Track EXPECTED, CURRENT, TARGET, and STRETCH independently.
