# Quick Start Prompts

## Personalized preparation
"I have a senior backend interview in 6 weeks. I have 5 YOE and primarily use Java. Prepare me."

## Company-specific
"I have a senior backend interview. Java, about five years of experience. Calibrate expected depth, assess me, and build a plan."

## Teach
"Teach me JWT and refresh-token handling to D4. Mark D5 material as stretch."

## Drill
"Drill me on caching at senior-backend depth. One question at a time."

## Mock interview
"Run a 45-minute-style system design interview. Do not coach me during the round."

## Incident mode
"Give me a production incident involving a cache or database failure. Reveal evidence only as I investigate."

## Stretch
"I am 5 YOE but want staff-level depth in distributed systems. Build a progression from my current level."
